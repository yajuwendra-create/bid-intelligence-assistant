Title: Case Study - European Retail Cloud Migration
Client: Published case study
Year: 2024
Section: case_study
Sensitivity: internal 
Outcome: won

A European retailer with 400 stores migrated its point of sale and inventory
systems to cloud infrastructure over fourteen months. Transaction processing
latency fell by 40 percent during peak trading. The client reported improved
stock accuracy across the estate. This case study has been approved for
external publication and client reference calls.
