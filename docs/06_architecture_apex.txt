Title: Security Architecture - Meridian Health
Year: 2024
Section: security_compliance

Our proposed security model moves away from traditional network perimeters.
Access decisions are made per request, based on verified identity, device
posture and contextual risk signals rather than network location. We refer to
this as an identity-based perimeterless security model.
