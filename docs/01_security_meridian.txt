Title: Security Architecture - Meridian Health
Client: Meridian Health
Year: 2024
Section: security_compliance
Sensitivity: internal 
Outcome: won

Our proposed security model moves away from traditional network perimeters.
Access decisions are made per request, based on verified identity, device
posture and contextual risk signals rather than network location. Every
service authenticates every call. We refer to this as an identity-based
perimeterless security model. All administrative access requires hardware
token verification. Audit logging is immutable and retained for seven years
to meet healthcare regulatory requirements.
