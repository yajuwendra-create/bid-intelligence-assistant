Title: Delivery Approach - Northwind Logistics
Client: Northwind Logistics
Year: 2023
Section: delivery_plan
Sensitivity: internal 
Outcome: won

Delivery follows a three-phase model. Phase one establishes the landing zone
and migrates two non-critical workloads to validate the approach. Phase two
migrates the core fleet management platform over nine weeks with parallel
running. Phase three decommissions legacy infrastructure. A dedicated
transition manager is embedded with the client throughout. Weekly steering
reviews track against agreed exit criteria for each phase.
